01

Everything it does

Reading memory is automatic. Scenes are on request.

01

The scroll offset is written about 600 ms after you stop, and again when the tab is hidden or closed. One record per page: no polling, no writes per pixel, no timer while you read.

02

A pill in the bottom-right corner offers to take you back, with the percentage reached. It never scrolls on its own — you click it, or you ignore it.

03

Positions below the trigger distance are not offered, so a page you barely scrolled stays quiet. The delay, the fade and that distance are adjustable with a licence.

04

Single-page apps keep separate positions per route: /app/inbox and /app/settings are different pages. Tracking parameters such as utm_*, fbclid and gclid are dropped from the key.

05

Lazy pages are handled. If the saved offset is beyond the current document height, the restore retries twice and then gives up quietly instead of fighting the layout.

06

Pages that hold state take a scene: the scroll offset plus every text field, textarea, select, checkbox, radio and open panel, saved under a name you choose. (Pro)

Also included

  • Restoring a scene always shows what will be overwritten first. That confirmation is free and on by default — a safety step is not an upsell.
  • Fields are found by structural path first, then id, then name, so a page that shifts slightly still restores. Values go back through the native setter with input and change events, so React and Vue notice.
  • Passwords, one-time codes, card numbers, CVV, IBAN and SSN are excluded at capture time, not filtered at restore time. There is no setting that re-enables them.
  • Twenty pages of history in the free version, 500 with a licence. The popup lists them with the percentage reached; click a row to reopen that page.
  • JSON export and import, free. Import merges rather than replaces, and keeps the newer record when both sides have the same page.
  • Six interface languages: English, 中文, 日本語, Deutsch, Español, Français.

02

Free vs Pro

Positions are free forever. Pro adds the part that writes back into pages.

Free

Automatic reading positions on every page, the continue pill, per-site pause, clear this page, 20 pages of history, SPA route tracking, the restore confirmation, and JSON export and import of positions.

Pro

Page scenes with save and restore, history up to 500 pages, adjustable pill delay, fade and trigger distance, regular expressions in the ignore list, several named scenes per page, read-only fields on request, and a keyboard shortcut.

The licence is optional. ReadPos keeps working offline and asks the licence server for a check about every two hours when you open the popup; if the check fails, nothing is locked. One key activates two browsers.

Private by design

Positions and scenes stay on your device. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Read exactly what is stored and when the extension uses the network.
